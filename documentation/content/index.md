Tarnished 0.2.1 keeps applications, leads, documents, interview rounds and
analytics on your own infrastructure. AI extraction, feedback and speech
transcription are optional.

## Start here

1. [Install with Docker Compose](./install/docker-compose.md) using SQLite.
2. [Create the first account](./get-started/create-admin-account.md).
3. [Add your first application](./get-started/create-your-first-application.md).

For other deployment environments, use [PostgreSQL Compose](./install/postgresql-docker-compose.md)
or [Helm](./install/helm.md).
